## Interpretation boundary

With a fixed 10 ms synthetic runner, throughput scaled from about 97 runs/s at
concurrency 1 to about 1,894 runs/s at concurrency 20 while all correctness gates
remained green. This supports only the statement that the supervisor itself did not
become the observed bottleneck for up to 100 synthetic accounts in this matrix.

It does not include PostgreSQL query/connection cost, lease and outbox transactions,
session decrypt, Teleproto connection memory, Telegram latency/FloodWait, network,
logging backend, or long-lived process growth. The sub-millisecond drain values occur
after synthetic runners have completed and cannot set a production termination grace
period. F5.7b and F5.7c remain mandatory before F5.7d chooses deployment numbers.
